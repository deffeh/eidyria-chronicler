### Tralen Idrendri
You are a member of the Censorate. Your official title is Under-Censor. You began your career as a Clerk at the Censorate headquarters in Tilsit Rein. Afterwards, you were an Apprentice Censor to Censor Hatharal Zumfaren, who is now your handler. You were recently promoted to this position and this is your first solo mission.

Your mission requires you to investigate and possibly recover a missing shipment. This shipment was to be transported from Falsruun to Tilsit Rein several days ago by the dwarves which on average takes about a full days travel. The guards were ambushed and shipment stolen just north of Tilsit Rein. You weren't told what the contents of this shipment are but you know that shipments between dwarven and elven settlements are a regular occurance. Usually rock, ore, and other valueable materials mined by the dwarves in exchange for commodities such as foodstuffs, timber, textiles, etc.

All the dwarves on the guard detail were killed except for one. The lone survivior is named Guyman Johanseburg. He was found unconsious at the scene of the ambush alongside four dead dwarves. At the time, you were still an Apprentice Censor. You and Censor Zumfaren were dispatched as soon as news reached Tilsit Rein. At the scene, you and Zumfaren found signs of a standard ambush with both magic and martial weapons being used from both parties. The attackers did not leave anything behind other than their footsteps going off the road into the woods to the west. Zumfaren used speak with dead to interrogate the dead dwarves. The general consensus was that the ambush occured in the early hours of the morning when it was still dark and carried out by hooded humanoid figures from the front (south) and the woods (west). Two of the dwarves who died towards the woods to the west mentioned seeing a humanoid, taller than the rest, squating about, further into the treeline who did not participate in the ambush. 

You and Zumfaren returned to Tilsit Rein, bringing in Guyman for further questioning. You found his accounts of the ambush in line with the accounts of the other dwarves and had no reason to suspect him of any wrongdoing. With other matters to attend to, Zumfaren could not continue this investigation. He received authorization from higher up, promoted you to Under-Censor, and tasked you to investigate and potentially recover this missing shipment. Guyman, under orders from his superiors, has been tasked to assist you in the form of a joint operation between dwarven and elven authorities.

The two of you departed later that day, returning to the ambush site and following the tracks into the woods. The shipment is heavy and it seems to be carried and at times dragged by only one person. There seem to be at least 5 other people and some sort of dog. You follow this trail over the course of three days taking you across the border into the Kingdom of Dagonti towards its capital Kumahrus. As you appraoch the city, the trail becomes cold, lost in the tracks of the hundrends of people who travel the roads. You decide to stay at a tavern after camping in the desert for the past several days.

### Zormenos
As a sorcerer you were born with innate magical abilities. This magic is divine, from your infernal blood and soul. The nature of the magic is evil but it is a magic you have complete control over.

After running away from being a priest, your abilities to disguse yourself as a human caught the attention of an "underground" tiefling organization called the Brotherhood. You were recently recruited by a tiefling named Valin to join the Brotherhood. You know that Valin is in the militant arm of the Brotherhood and has been in the organization for some time. Higher ups in the organization were interested in your sorcerous abilities but more importantly your ability to disguise yourself and blend in with human society.

In recent months, there have been growing rumors coming from the south, that tiefling families and individuals have been disapearing with some even saying abducted. This rumor has been spreading by word of mouth with signs indicating that the rumors are originating from within the Lavent-Neshian Empire. Because of your abilities to blend into human society, the Brotherhood has sent you on a mission to the south of the continent to observe and potentially investigate these rumors. Because you have only recently joined the Brotherhood, they have sent Valin along with you as a handler.

### Valin

You feel another influence and power from this plane, specifically this continent. This mysterious item you feel is what granted you your powers when you were born. You feel a strong connection to it, even stronger than your infernal connection. You feel this connection in physical space with it becoming more directionally accurate and stronger as you grew up. When you were a child, you felt this connection all around you. As you begain training your martial hexblade ablities and embraced the warlock path, it became clear that the connection came from the south of the continent. With your travels and experence in recent life, you are now certain that connection is coming from within the Lavent-Neshian Empire.

At some point in your life, you joined the an "underground" tiefling organization called the Brotherhood. You are in the militant arm of the organization and have led a small squad in the past. You have participated in raids, defenses, and patrols of all sorts.

In recent months, there have been growing rumors coming from the south, that tiefling families and individuals have been disapearing with some even saying abducted. This rumor has been spreading by word of mouth with signs indicating that the rumors are originating from within the Lavent-Neshian Empire.

### Grommak
A few weeks ago when you were in Pereville, some human men approached you who were amazed by your size. They wanted to offer you a job. They said they wanted you to first travel with them for about a week and then they would need you to carry something heavy and travel with them for another week back home. They said they would pay you a lot, more money than you've ever had before. They also had a large mastiff that you liked. You accepted, mainly because of the mastiff.

You traveled for about week going east across the Kingdom of Dagonti, first stopping at Nandraa, then Kumahrus, and eventually the border of Asfallia. Before you arrived at the border, your companions decided to go north before crossing the river and going into the woods of Asfallia. You traveled eastward for another day before setting camp in woods. In the middle of that night you were awoken by your companions and they took you on a walk in the woods. Eventually they told you to sit and wait behind a rock while they went on ahead. Minutes later, you hear yelling and screaming and sounds of fighting and magic in the direction your companions went. Moments after, they told you to follow them quickly and to go pick up a large chest. As you arrived to the scene of all the commotion, you come to a road that cut through the woods and you see a chest laying on the ground and 4 dead dwarves around it. You picked it up and you and your companions ran into the woods back to your camp. You broke camp and traveled nonstop for 4 days going westward retracing your original route arriving at Kumarhus at the brink of exhaustion where you and your companions decide to stop at a tavern for the night.
