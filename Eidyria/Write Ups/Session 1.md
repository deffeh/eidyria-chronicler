# Session 1
The Lazy Cup is one of many inns in the city of Kumahrus. The small one story inn is situated near the the city center and markets, making it one of the more popular inns for travelers and merchants. As the sun was setting one summer evening, a group of weary travelers, consisting of five humans, an orc, and a dog approached the inn. The humans, all men with varying shades of dark hair and olive skin were accompanied by a large male orc with pale greyish green skin who stood over them by at least a head's height. Clearly an outsider of the group, the orc carried a large crate over his shoulders lagging behind the others and following them wherever they went.

As the group arrived at the front doors of the inn, one of the humans turned around to the orc and said, *"Hey Grom, get that crate around back and meet us inside."* The others went into the inn along with the dog as Grom took the crate around the side and to the back of the establishment where many of the other guests left their larger belongings. 

Coming inside, Grom found his companions seated together at the bar across the common room. Still early in the evening, the inn was still mostly quiet and empty with only a handfull of other guests other than Grom and his companions. At the bar, a plate of tonight's stew and a pitcher of ale lay waiting for Grom. As he walked across the room, he drew the eyes of several guests and empleyees of the inn. Once seated, Grom began devouring his meal paying no heed to his environment. His companions continued on with their own food and conversations amongst themselves.

Several minutes later, the door of the inn suddenly opened and two tiefling men entered the inn. One with purplish ingido skin and the other light red with strangely no horns unlike his companion. The innkeeper apprached them with an eyebrow raised briefly in suspicion. *"Would you like a bed tonight? Food and drink are included for 1 gold piece."* asked the woman. The two men each handed their gold piece to the lady and made their way to the bar, sitting across from Grom and his companions. The two quietly conversed and kept to themselves.

Over the next several minutes, the common room began populating with guests, mostly human, though there were two dragonborn merchants in the corner of the room.

Once again, the door of the inn suddenly opened and two figures stood in the doorway. One was tall and the other stood several feet shorter. Upon entering the building, the tall figure was revealed to be an male elf of blonde hair and fair skin in chainmail armor. Accompanying him was a middle aged male dwarf with greying strawberry blonde hair. The innkeeper moved to greet them, again with an eyebrow raised briefly. *"Would you like a bed tonight? Food and drink are included for 1 gold piece."* repeated the woman. The elf looked at the inkeeper nervously, glanced at his companion, then back to the woman, and handed over a gold piece. The dwarf followed suit and the two of them made their way to the bar.

Walking over to the bar, the elf scanned the room eventually locking his eyes on Grom sitting there, at this point finishing his fourth pitcher of ale. Nudging his companion, the elf remarked, *"Hey, could this be...?"* 

*"I can't tell."* replied the dwarf.

The pair sat down at the front and center of the bar with their backs to the door, separating the two earlier groups of people. The elf, now sitting adjacent to Grom, seemed more interested in him than in his own meal. Noticing the elf's poor attempt to hide his gawking, Grom turned toward him and sneered, *"Knife-Ears"* before returning to his drink.

People continued trickling in as the minutes passed, many of whom sat alone or in small groups amongst the smaller tables spread throughout. Some stood around the fire pit at the center of the room as the desert night began to grow cold. The volume of the room grew louder with conversations and laughter as people settled in for the night.

The bar, now quite packed, left little room for new guests at the counter. However, this didn't stop a man from squeezing himself in between the elf and dwarf. The man was a human with tan skin and dark brown hair wearing studded leather armor. After the man asserted his position at the counter, the elf turned to him asking, *"What'd you do that for? Can't you see we're sitting here?"*

*"I did it because I wanted to."* replied the man.

With the conversation clearly going nowhere, the elf returned to his meal and observation of Grom.

Several minutes later, a beautiful girl with fair skin, dark hair, and a revealing dress approached the bar from behind the elf and the human. Smiling, she slipped between the two and draped her arms over each of their shoulders as she leaned in playfully. As she stroked the human's arms with one hand and the elf's pointy ear with the other, she asked, *"What brings you strong men to the city?"* The elf ignored the girl's question, removed the her hand from his ear, and resumed looking at the orc conspicuously. The girl turned her head toward the human, looking at him expectantly. The man asked, *"Do you have any diseases?"* 

*"I don't have anything unless you want something darling."* she replied jokingly. *"Not interested."* stated the man as he began ignoring her advances. Rolling her eyes, she moved on around the bar attempting to flirt and solicit her services.

She came over to the tieflings first, stating, *"You know, I've never been with two tieflings at the same time before."* The tieflings ignored her outright. Moving on to the other side of the bar, she stopped beside Grom, asking him, *"Are you as big down there as you are up top?"* Grom replied simply, *"I have big feet."* Rolling her eyes once more, she continued down the counter to Grom's companions. There she met a much more receptive reaction. One of the men asked her, *"How much are you, honey?"*

*"Five gold pieces for the night, and I'll let you do whatever you want to me."* she replied.

The man pulled out a coin purse and produced five gold coins. The man handed the girl the coins as she climbed onto his lap. Those that were perceptive noticed that these gold coins were Golden Eagles, gold pieces minted by the Lavent-Neshian Empire.

As the Grom's companions finished their meals and drinks, they turned to Grom saying, *"Keep an eye on our things. We're going out tonight and we'll be back later."* After a nod of acknowledgement from Grom, his companions stood up along with the girl and together they all walked across the room, out the front door, and into the dark street.


# Session 1
The Lazy Cup was one of many inns in the city of Kumahrus. Situated near the city center and its busy markets, the small one-story establishment was a popular destination for merchants, travelers, and caravans passing through the capital.

As the sun set on a warm summer evening, a group of weary travelers approached the inn. The party consisted of five human men, an orc, and a large dog. The humans all shared similar features, olive skin and dark hair in varying shades, but the orc stood apart from them. Towering over the others by more than a head, he possessed pale grey-green skin and carried a large wooden crate across his shoulders. He followed several paces behind the group, making it clear he was not one of them.

When they reached the entrance, one of the men glanced back at the orc. *"Hey, Grom. Get that crate around back and meet us inside."* The others entered the inn with the dog while Grom carried the crate around the side of the building to a storage area in the back where guests left wagons, cargo, and other bulky possessions.

By the time he entered through the front door, his companions had already claimed seats together at the bar. The common room was still relatively quiet, with only a handful of patrons scattered among the tables. A plate of stew and a pitcher of ale had been left waiting for him at the counter. As Grom crossed the room, he drew curious glances from several guests and even a few members of the staff. Taking his seat, he immediately began devouring his meal, paying little attention to his surroundings. His companions continued their own meals and conversations as though he were not there at all.

Several minutes later, the inn's front door opened and two tiefling men stepped inside. One possessed purplish-indigo skin and a pair of prominent horns, while the other had light red skin and, strangely, no horns at all. The innkeeper approached them with a practiced smile, though one eyebrow rose briefly as she took in the unusual pair. *"Need a room?"* she asked. *"Food and drink are included. One gold piece."* Each tiefling produced a coin. After securing their rooms, they settled into seats at the bar across from Grom and his companions. Aside from a few quiet words exchanged between them, they kept to themselves.

As the evening wore on, more travelers filtered into the common room. Most were human, though two dragonborn merchants occupied a corner table, speaking quietly over mugs of ale.

Not long after, the door opened once again. A pair of travelers entered. One was tall and slender, the other several feet shorter and considerably broader. As they stepped into the light of the common room, their features became clear. The taller figure was an elf with fair skin, blond hair, and chainmail armor. Beside him walked a middle-aged dwarf whose strawberry-blond beard had begun to gray with age. The innkeeper greeted them as she had every guest that evening. *"Need a room? Food and drink are included. One gold piece."* The elf hesitated, glancing nervously at his companion before handing over a coin. The dwarf followed suit. With that done, the pair made their way toward the bar. As he crossed the room, the elf's eyes wandered over the patrons before eventually settling on Grom. The orc sat at the counter finishing his fourth pitcher of ale. The elf nudged the dwarf. *"Hey... could this be him?"* The dwarf studied Grom for a moment. *"I can't tell."*

They took seats at the center of the bar, unintentionally placing themselves between the tieflings and Grom's group. Once seated, the elf seemed far more interested in the orc than in the meal placed before him. Every few moments his eyes drifted back in Grom's direction. Eventually, Grom noticed. Turning slowly in his seat, he fixed the elf with a glare and curled his lip into a sneer. *"Knife-ears."* Without waiting for a response, he turned back to his drink.

As the minutes passed, more patrons filtered into the common room. Some gathered around the smaller tables scattered throughout the inn, while others stood near the fire pit at the center of the room, seeking warmth as the desert night grew colder. Conversations multiplied, laughter became more frequent, and before long the once-quiet inn was filled with the sounds of travelers settling in for the evening.

The bar soon became crowded, leaving little room for newcomers. That did not stop one man from forcing his way between the elf and the dwarf. He was a human with tan skin, dark brown hair, and a suit of worn studded leather armor. The elf frowned at him. *"What'd you do that for? Can't you see we're sitting here?"* The man shrugged. *"I did it because I wanted to."* Realizing the conversation was unlikely to go anywhere, the elf returned to his meal and his occasional glances toward Grom.

Several minutes later, a beautiful young woman with fair skin, dark hair, and a revealing dress approached the bar from behind the elf and the human. Smiling, she slipped between them and draped an arm over each of their shoulders. Leaning in close, she idly stroked the human's arm with one hand while tracing a finger along the elf's pointed ear with the other. *"What brings two strong men to the city?"* she purred. The elf ignored her completely. He removed her hand from his ear and resumed staring at Grom. The woman turned expectantly toward the human. *"Do you have any diseases?"* he asked. She blinked in surprise before laughing. *"Not unless you're looking for one, darling."* *"Not interested,"* the man replied flatly. The smile vanished from her face.

With an exasperated sigh, she pushed away from the pair and continued down the bar in search of more promising customers. She stopped beside the two tieflings. *"You know,"* she said with a playful grin, *"I've never been with two tieflings at the same time before."* Neither tiefling acknowledged her existence.

Undeterred, she moved on. Stopping beside Grom, she looked him up and down. *"Tell me, are you as big down there as you are up top?"* *"I have big feet,"* Grom replied. The woman stared at him for a moment before shaking her head and continuing along the counter.

At last she reached Grom's companions, who proved considerably more receptive. *"How much are you, honey?"* one of them asked. *"Five gold pieces for the night,"* she replied with a smile, *"and I'll let you do whatever you want."* The man produced a coin purse and counted out five gold pieces. As he handed them over and the woman climbed onto his lap, the observant might have noticed the coins themselves. They were Golden Eagles, gold pieces minted by the Lavent-Neshian Empire.

Eventually Grom's companions finished their meals and drinks. One of them turned to the orc. *"Keep an eye on our things. We're going out tonight. We'll be back later."* Grom gave a simple nod. The men rose from their seats, the woman accompanying them. Together they crossed the crowded common room, passed through the front door, and disappeared into the darkness beyond.
