# Session 2
In the front of the Grom's former companions stood the human who scuffled with Valindor earlier. Grom knew him to be Conras, the leader of the group. Conras spent several moments scanning the room before locking his eyes with Valindor's. Upon meeting his gaze, his eyes widened with rage. Pointing towards the indigo tiefling, Conras announced, *"YOU'RE A DEAD MAN!"* Conras stormed across the lobby of the inn straight towards the indigo tiefling with his companions following close behind.

*"Woah! Woah! Let's talk this out."* said Valindor, attempting to defuse the situation. Failing to be persuaded, Conras continued forward as he cocked his arm back preparing to throw a punch at the tiefling's head. Not wanting to escalate, Valindor did not react as the man began to throw his punch. Still drunk, Conras stumbled and missed the punch.

Tralen stepped forward beside Valindor saying, *"Now, now. We can discuss this like civilized men or we can take this outside as to not disturb the patrons of this establishment."* Still ignoring the things being said to him, Conras yelled at Valindor, *"Give us our shit back! We know your little girl friend took it!"* *"Speaking of your things, why don't you tell us about this crate of yours around back"* replied Tralen. *"What's ours is none of your concern. Now where is our fucking shit?"*

Reaching the limit of his patience, Tralen drew his longsword, turned to party saying, *"Keep them alive."* before striking Conras in the face with the guard and pommel of his weapon. Immediately, everyone drew their weapons and positioned to fight. As Conras drew his shortsword, Valindor quickly threw a knife into his foot pinning it the the wooden floor. He screamed from the pain before thrusting his blade towards Tralen. The blade connected, piercing into the Tralen's upper arm. Ferbert stepped forward to left of Conras and threw a right hook, connecting square with the side of his head. Guyman, following behind Ferbert drove the butt end of his maul into the Conras's crotch with a quick reverse thrust. The man instantly collapsed, falling unconsious on the floor.

A small bolt flew across the room and embedded itself in Ferbert's face before he fell lifelessly. Following the trajectory of the projectile, it came from a hand-crossbow wielded by one of the naked men in the back. 

Zormenos moved along the wall past the firepit that partially separated the two groups to flank from the left. He raised his right hand and whispered a prayer. A bolt of pure radiance lanced through the air toward the other naked man before striking him squarely in the crotch, erupting in a shower of radiant sparks. As the light faded, a soft golden aura clung to the man, tracing his silhouette.

# Session 2
At the front of Grom's former companions stood the human who had scuffled with Valindor earlier. Grom knew him as Conras, the leader of the group. Conras paused in the doorway, his eyes sweeping across the common room. One by one, he took in its occupants until his gaze settled on Valindor. For a heartbeat, neither man moved. Then recognition flashed across Conras's face. His expression twisted with fury as he thrust a finger toward the indigo tiefling. *"YOU'RE A DEAD MAN!"* Conras stormed across the common room toward Valindor, his companions surging after him.

*"Woah, woah. Let's talk this out!"* Valindor said, raising his hands in a placating gesture. Conras paid him no heed. Snarling, he drew back his fist and lunged forward, aiming a wild punch at the tiefling's head. Determined not to escalate the confrontation, Valindor stood his ground and made no attempt to retaliate. Still unsteady from drink, Conras lost his footing as he threw the punch. His fist cut harmlessly through the air, and he stumbled past his intended target.

Tralen stepped forward to stand beside Valindor, raising a hand in an attempt to calm the situation. *"Now, now. We can discuss this like civilized men, or we can settle this outside where we won't disturb the other guests."* Conras paid him no attention. His glare remained fixed on Valindor. *"Give us our shit back!"* he shouted. *"We know your little girlfriend took it!"* *"Speaking of your belongings,"* Tralen replied evenly, *"perhaps you'd like to tell us about the crate behind the inn."* Conras finally turned his attention to the elf, his expression hardening. *"What's ours is none of your concern."* He jabbed a finger toward Valindor. *"Now where's our fucking shit?"*

Reaching the end of his patience, Tralen drew his longsword and glanced at his companions. *"Keep them alive."* Without another word, he stepped forward and drove the crossguard and pommel of his sword into Conras's face. The common room erupted into chaos. Weapons flashed from their sheaths as both groups rushed into motion. Clutching his bloodied nose, Conras drew his shortsword, but before he could take another step, Valindor flicked a throwing dagger that was hidden behind his back. The blade buried itself through the man's foot and into the wooden floor, pinning him in place. Conras cried out in pain but refused to yield. With a furious roar, he thrust his shortsword at Tralen, the blade biting into the elf's upper arm.
Before he could recover, Ferbert stepped in from Conras's left and drove a heavy right hook into the side of his jaw. A heartbeat later, Guyman surged up behind Ferbert. Gripping his maul low on the haft, he drove the butt end upward in a quick reverse thrust into Conras's groin. The fight left him instantly. Conras crumpled to the floor unconscious, his shortsword slipping from his grasp.

A crossbow bolt whistled across the common room and buried itself in Ferbert's face. He crumpled to the floor without a sound. Following the bolt's path, the party's eyes found one of the naked men standing at the rear of the room, a hand crossbow still raised in his hands.

Zormenos slipped along the wall, circling the fire pit that partially divided the two groups until he reached the enemy's right flank. Raising his right hand, he whispered a brief prayer. A lance of radiant energy burst from his fingertips and streaked across the common room, striking the other naked man square in the crotch. The impact exploded in a shower of golden light, forcing him to double over in agony. As the brilliance faded, a faint golden aura clung to his body, outlining his silhouette and marking him for the next strike.
