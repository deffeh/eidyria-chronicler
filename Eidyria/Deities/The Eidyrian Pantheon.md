# The Eidyrian Pantheon
The Eidyrian Pantheon, more commonly known as "The Gods," are the deities that are worshiped all across Eidyria. It's adherents believe that the world is governed by six deities who embody the fundamental forces of the world and grant divine power to their followers but rarely, if ever, interact directly in mortal affairs. The names of the Gods are mortal inventions that predate the First Era. Their true names, if they possess such things, are unknown, and the Gods are believed to be wholly indifferent to how they are addressed by the peoples of the world.
