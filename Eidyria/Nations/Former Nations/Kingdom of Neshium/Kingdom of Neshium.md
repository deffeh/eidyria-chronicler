# Kingdom of Neshium
The Kingdom of Neshium, formally The Lands of the Neshian Crown, are the Neshian territories of the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]]. The territory reaches from the ... Mountains in the north to the [[Great Sea]] in the south. The capital is the city of Neshium.
