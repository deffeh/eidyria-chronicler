# Kingdom of Neshium
The Kingdom of Neshium, formally The Lands of the Neshian Crown, are the Neshian territories of the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]]. The territory reaches from the [[Lesser Calcare Mountains]] in the north to the [[Great Sea]] in the south. The [[Kingdom of Lavencia]], the northern lands of the Dual Monarchy, lay to the north of the [[Lesser Calcare Mountains]]. The capital is the city of Neshium.

## History
### Before the First Era
Presence of ancient orc tribes within the modern borders of the Kingdom of Neshium dates back to 8,000 BFE where the first orcs migrated from the center of the peninsula and settled along the coast. 
### First Era (1E)
Advances in shipbuilding and navigation during the 2nd century enabled seafaring humans from northern Eidyria to begin exploring and raiding the coasts of the [[Viden Peninsula]]. While the earliest migrations were concentrated along the northern coast, subsequent expeditions sailed west around the peninsula and established themselves along the south-eastern coasts during the third and fourth centuries. Over the following centuries the humans slowly expanded their territory inland and created a distinct cultural identity separate from the other humans settling the north of the peninsula. The 4th century was characterised by frequent conflict between humans and orcs, whos resistance significantly hindered territorial expansion. These conflicts were intensified by the arrival of orcish populations displaced by human expansion in the north. Although human settlements had been established further inland, control over the surrounding countryside remained limited, leaving many settlements isolated from one another and territories politically fragmented. The insecurity of overland routes frequently disrupted trade and the movement of supplies, leaving isolated communities vulnerable to shortages and famine. As a result, numerous settlements were abandoned or destroyed, while others struggled to maintain their populations in the face of continued orc attacks.

By the 5th century, [[Neshium]] had emerged as one of the principal human settlements along the southeastern coast, developing into an independent city-state through maritime trade and the consolidation of neighboring coastal communities. Its growing economic and military strength enabled it to extend its influence beyond the immediate coastline, although much of the interior remained under the control of orc tribes. Over the following two centuries, Neshium gradually expanded its territory through a series of military campaigns against the orcs and the incorporation of smaller human settlements. By the 7th century, this process had brought much of the southeastern interior under Neshian rule, culminating in the establishment of the kingdom in 1E 679 under Corentin I.
### Second Era (2E)
The successive deaths of King Lorent III and Queen Joralia in 2E 112 plunged the kingdom into a succession crisis, weakening royal authority and creating divisions among the Neshian nobility. The political instability provided an opportunity for King Mercian of the [[Kingdom of Lavencia]], who had long sought to unite the peninsula under Lavent rule.

In 2E 112, Mercian invaded Neshium with a large army, rapidly conquering much of the kingdom and forcing the remaining Neshian forces to retreat toward the capital. By 2E 114, his forces had reached the city of [[Neshium]] and placed it under siege. The siege was briefly lifted later that year when orc raids forced Mercian to divert part of his army, but was subsequently resumed. In 2E 115, the city fell to Lavent forces, bringing an end to Neshium's independence.

Following the conquest, Mercian assumed the Neshian crown rather than incorporating the kingdom directly into Lavencia. Neshium retained its separate laws, nobility, and governing institutions, although ultimate authority now rested with the Lavent monarch. The resulting personal union brought the two kingdoms under a single sovereign while preserving their distinct political identities. This arrangement eventually developed into the Dual Monarchy, in which Neshium was formally recognized as a constituent kingdom alongside Lavencia.
