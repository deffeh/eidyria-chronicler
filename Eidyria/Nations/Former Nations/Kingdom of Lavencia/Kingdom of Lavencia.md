# Kingdom of Lavencia
The Kingdom of Lavencia, officially The Kingdoms and Lands Represented in the Imperial Authority, is the northern part of the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]], the Dual Monarchy created in 2E 115 following the conquest of Neshium. This name for the region is a common, but unofficial one.

The Lavent capital is the city of Lavencia. The territory has a population of about 1 million. It reaches from the borders of the [[Union of Kinot]] and [[Könika]] in the east to the [[Kande Ocean]] in the west, as well as the [[Gulf of Kinot]] in the north to the ... Mountains in the south. It comprises the former city-states of [[Lavencia]], [[Zorino]], [[Dionadyr]], and [[Wostend]], along with their surrounding territories.
