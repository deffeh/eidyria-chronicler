# Kingdom of Lavencia
The Kingdom of Lavencia, officially The Kingdoms and Lands Represented in the Imperial Authority, is the northern part of the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]], the Dual Monarchy created in 2E 115 following the conquest of Neshium. This name for the region is a common, but unofficial one.

The Lavent capital is the city of Lavencia. The territory has a population of about 1 million, nearly 85% of the empire's population. It reaches from the borders of the [[Union of Kinot]] and [[Könika]] in the east to the [[Kande Ocean]] in the west, as well as the [[Gulf of Kinot]] in the north to the [[Lesser Calcare Mountains]] in the south. It comprises the former city-states of [[Lavencia]], [[Zorino]], [[Dionadyr]], and [[Wostend]], along with their surrounding territories.

## History
### Before the First Era
During the pre-human era, the region of what is now the Kingdom of Lavencia was settled by varius orc tribes and had served as the core of ancient orcish culture. Near the city of [[Dionadyr]] lies the oldest archaeological evidence of the orcs in [[Eidyria]] dating back to 8,000 BFE.

### First Era (1E)
The advancement of sailing ships and navigation in the 2nd century allowed seafaring human warriors from northern Eidyria to raid the coasts of the [[Viden Peninsula]]. These groups then began to settle in increasing numbers over the course of the second and third centuries, initially in the northern part of the country. Their advance was contained for some decades after the orcs' victory at the [[Battle of Bagno]], but subsequently resumed, overrunning the fertile lowlands of the [[Viden Peninsula]] and reducing the area under orcish control to a series of separate enclaves in the more rugged country to the east by the end of the 4th century.

During the settlement period the lands ruled by the incomers seem to have been fragmented into numerous tribal territories, but by the 4th century, these had coalesced into several independent city-states including [[Lavencia]], [[Zorino]], [[Dionadyr]], and [[Wostend]]. Over the following centuries, this process of political consolidation continued. The 5th century saw a struggle for hegemony between Zorino and Lavencia, which in the 6th century gave way to Lavent preeminence. Later in that century escalating attacks by the orcs culminated in the sacks of Zorino and Dionadyr and the fall of the east. Lavencia and Wostend, under Lavent King Alvren, were left as the only surviving human cities, and under his successors, the kingdom steadily reconquered the east at the expense of the returning orc tribes. This brought about the political unification of the Kingdom of Lavencia, first accomplished under King Asteran and definitively established after further conflict by King Edranis in 1E 653.

### Second Era (2E)
The [[Infernal Reaping]] presented one of the greatest challenges to the Kingdom of Lavencia during the early Second Era. The sudden appearance of infernal characteristics among the kingdom's population provoked widespread religious controversy, with many interpreting the transformations as a manifestation of divine punishment or infernal corruption. Religious authorities increasingly condemned the afflicted, while popular fears gave rise to accusations of witchcraft, demonic influence, and other forms of heresy.

As public hostility intensified, local nobles and magistrates began imposing restrictions upon those affected by the Reaping. Tieflings were increasingly excluded from public office, military service, and certain professions, while incidents of mob violence and forced expulsions became more frequent. Although the Crown initially adopted an inconsistent approach, mounting pressure from religious institutions and influential noble families eventually led to the formalization of discriminatory laws throughout the kingdom.

By the 2nd century, much of the kingdom's tiefling population had either been driven from their communities or compelled to live under increasingly restrictive conditions. Many fled eastward through [[Könika]] to seek refuge in the [[Kingdom of Dagonti]], where attitudes toward the afflicted were generally more tolerant. However, the journey through Könika was particularly dangerous, as refugees were frequently attacked by hostile orc tribes. Many perished along the way, while others were forced to turn back or seek refuge in isolated settlements. The persecution of tieflings contributed to the development of a more exclusionary Lavent identity, establishing social and political attitudes that would endure long after the formation of the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]].

The unification of the peninsula through the conquest of the neighboring [[Kingdom of Neshium]] had long been an ambition of King Mercian. An opportunity arose in 2E 112 following the successive deaths of King Lorent III and Queen Joralia of Neshium, which left the kingdom politically vulnerable.

Taking advantage of the succession crisis, Mercian assembled an army and invaded the Kingdom of Neshium, rapidly bringing much of the kingdom under Lavent control. In 2E 114, his forces laid siege to Neshium, although the campaign was briefly interrupted later that year by orc raids that forced Mercian to divert part of his army. Following the resumption of the siege, Lavent forces ultimately captured Neshium in 2E 115, bringing the kingdom under Mercian's control. Rather than annexing its territories directly, Mercian assumed the Neshian crown while preserving the kingdom's separate legal and political institutions.

The conquest consequently established a union of the two crowns, with Mercian reigning as king of both the Kingdoms of Lavencia and Neshium. Although united under a single sovereign, the kingdoms retained their distinct identities, laws, and governing institutions. This arrangement laid the foundations for the eventual establishment of the Dual Monarchy, under which the two kingdoms were formally recognized as constituent partners of a common monarchy.
