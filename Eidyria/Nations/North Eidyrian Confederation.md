# North Eidyrian Confederation
The North Eidyrian Confederation is mostly an expanse of forest and mountains divided into multiple minor kingdoms. It is bordered by the Kingdom of Berentsven to the west and the Kingdom of Seneaux to the south. The Confederation came into existence in 2E 153, three years after the invasion of the Könika region by the Lavent-Neshian Empire. The former smaller kingdoms of Seymon, Talov, and Belaya feared that, without mutual defence and integrated economies, they would be subject to foreign influence and invasion. The high king is selected from among the constituent kings and holds power over foreign relations and limited authority over the other kingdoms.
