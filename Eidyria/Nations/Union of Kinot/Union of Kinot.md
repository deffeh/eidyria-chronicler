# Union of Kinot
The Union of Kinot is a country in Central Eidyria. Situated in a coastal lowland region of the Heartlands, it is bordered by the [[Kingdom of Seneaux]] to the east, the [[Kingdom of Dagonti]], [[Könika]], and the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]] to the south, and the [[Gulf of Kinot]] to the west. The Union covers an area of 6,030 square miles (15,620 sq km) and has a population of more than 250 thousand. The capital and largest city is Chapilton; other major towns are Bilibine, Pereville, and Ulemoor.

The Union of Kinot is a stateless gerontocracy with a decentralized system structured around autonomous communities and local councils of elders. The Union is also also home to two main racial and linguistic communities: the Halflings which constitutes about 80 percent of the population and the Gnomes which constitutes about 20 percent of the population.
