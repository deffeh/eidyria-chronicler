# Falsruun
Falsruun, officially Falsruun City of the Lorenic Order, is a semi-autonomous theocratic city-state located in the [[Duns]] in northern [[Asfällia]]. It consists of the city as well as nearly 100 other small localities in the surrounding areas. With a population of ~22,000, it is the eighth largest urban area in Eidyria. The city in its modern form, was established by the Lorenic Order in 368 BFE, per the terms of Article 4 of the [[Hammer and Leaf Accord]], following the end of the [[Dwarven-Elven Conflict]].

Although predominantly dwarven populated, the territory is bound by the imposed union with Asfällia, which covered foreign policy and defense. Additionally, Asfällia was granted certain rights related to the mining facilities in the city.

Falsruun is one of the world's oldest cities, with its recorded history spanning over 21,000 years, and its earliest dwarven presence beginning somewhere between the 30th and 27th millennia BFE. 

## Etymology
According to ancient elvish scholars, the city was named Falsruun, meaning "Mountain Realm", by the first elves in their language, Sylvan.

## Lorenic Order
The Lorenic Order is a religious institution founded as a military society of the Lorenic faith in 368 BFE. The order was formed to protect mines, escort shipments, oversee engineering and metallurgy, and preserve sacred knowledge. It was established as a result of regime change following the Dwarven-Elven Conflict, replacing the [[Greathand Dynasty]]. The order's compliance with the [[Hammer and Leaf Accord]] leads some, to this day, to maintain that the Lorenic regime is an illegal government run by traitors.
