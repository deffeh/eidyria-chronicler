# Könika
Könika, formally the Occupied Könikan Territories, is a Lavent-Neshian zone of occupation established after the invasion of Könika by the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]] in 2E 165. It lies between the [[Rosso Mountains]] to the north and the [[Great Sea]] to the south. Könika borders the [[Lavent-Neshian Empire]] to the west, the [[Kingdom of Dagonti]] to the east, and the [[Union of Kinot]] to the north.

Könika is run by Laventcia-Neshium as a separate administrative unit for logistical purposes. The entirety of the occupied territory was divided into three districts named after and surrounding their respective settlements (Chuki, Pelrug, Vankrem). The administration is composed entirely of human Lavent-Neshian officials, with the intent that the area is to be colonized by human settlers who would reduce the local orc population to the level of serfs before their eventual genocide.
