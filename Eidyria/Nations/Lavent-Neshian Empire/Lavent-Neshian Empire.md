# Lavent-Neshian Empire
The Lavent-Neshian Empire, also referred to as Lavencia-Neshium and officially as the Lavent-Neshian Monarchy, is a multi-national empire in Western Eidyria. A military and diplomatic alliance, it consists of two sovereign states with a single monarch who is titled Emperor of Lavencia and King of Neshium. Following the conquest of Neshium by the Kingdom of Lavencia in 2E 115, King Mercian of Lavencia was crowned King of Neshium.

The Lavent-Neshian Empire is the most populous country in Eidyria and the fourth-largest in area (after the [[North Eidyrian Confederation]]).

At its core is the dual monarchy, which is a real union between the [[Kingdom of Lavencia]] and the [[Kingdom of Neshium]]. Following the 2E 115 reforms, the Lavent and Neshian states have since been co-equal in power. The two countries conduct unified diplomatic, defence, and monetary policies. For these purposes, "common" ministries of foreign affairs and defence are maintained under the monarch's direct authority, as is a third finance ministry responsible only for financing the two "common" portfolios as well as the Imperial Bank.

## Politics
### Imperial Authority
The Imperial Authority is the governing body which advises the emperor and institutes policy at his command. Members are appointed to and dismissed from their position by the emperor who serves as head of the authority. Only the emperor can make the authority's decisions into law.

### Institute
The Institute is a secret police organization used as an intelligence agency for the empire. It began in 1E 849 as a courier service in the Kingdom of Lavencia and developed into a spying agency. The organization also unofficially carries out assassinations and other subversive activities through a group called the Veiled Choir. The Institute is headquartered out of Lavencia and reports directly to the Imperial Authority.

### Veiled Choir
The Veiled Choir is an unofficial covert action group operating under the Institute. Though officially unrecognized by the Imperial government, the organization is widely believed to conduct assassinations, espionage, and political suppression both within and beyond Imperial borders. Due to the extreme secrecy surrounding the organization, little is publicly known about its structure, leadership, or true objectives, though persistent rumors have linked the Choir to the disappearance of dissidents, minorities, and isolated communities throughout the empire.
