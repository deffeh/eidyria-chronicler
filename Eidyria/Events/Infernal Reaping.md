# Infernal Reaping
The Infernal Reaping was a sudden and unexplained event in 1E 896, marking the beginning of the Second Era, during which humans across Eidyria developed infernal traits and became the first tieflings. The transformations appeared to occur simultaneously among unrelated individuals and families across the continent, with no clear pattern of geography or social status. Accounts described the event as brief, terrifying, and painful: a ring of magical fire appeared around the afflicted, black ichor rose from their feet and covered their bodies, and horns and tails emerged before the ichor receded and the flames vanished. Estimates suggest that several tens of thousands of people were transformed. No consensus exists regarding the cause of the Infernal Reaping, and it remains one of the most enduring mysteries in Eidyrian history.

In the years that followed, affected families were frequently regarded with suspicion and fear. Many believed that those who had undergone the transformation had commited some unknown transgression and were being or punished by infernal forces. Anti-tiefling sentiment intensified throughout much of the continent, particularly within the Lavent-Neshian Empire. Imperial authorities gradually enacted policies restricting tiefling residence, property ownership, and movement before eventually ordering the forced relocation and deportation of many tiefling populations from major settlements and urban centers. Large numbers of tieflings disappeared into rural regions, isolated communities, and underground networks to avoid persecution, while others fled beyond Imperial borders entirely. In northern regions of Eidyria, tieflings were not outlawed, though they remained heavily distrusted and socially ostracized. Even in states where no formal persecution existed, tieflings often faced discrimination, exclusion from religious and civic life, and widespread suspicion regarding their infernal origins. Although attitudes varied by region and period, the stigma associated with the Infernal Reaping persists to the present day.

### The Brotherhood
The Brotherhood is a decentralized continent-wide organization dedicated to the protection and advancement of tiefling communities. Founded in 2E 66 in response to decades of discrimination following the Infernal Reaping, it provides advocacy, mutual aid, and, when necessary, armed defense against persecution and abuse. Though viewed by its supporters as a vital protector of tiefling rights, its critics often regard it as a secretive and politically subversive movement.
