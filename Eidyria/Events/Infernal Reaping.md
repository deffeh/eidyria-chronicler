### The Brotherhood
The Brotherhood is a decentralized continent-wide organization dedicated to the protection and advancement of tiefling communities. Founded in response to centuries of discrimination following the Infernal Reaping, it provides advocacy, mutual aid, and, when necessary, armed defense against persecution and abuse. Though viewed by its supporters as a vital protector of tiefling rights, its critics often regard it as a secretive and politically subversive movement.
